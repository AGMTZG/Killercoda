### Using Helm Helper Templates for a Database Application

Your manager has asked you to standardize how Kubernetes resources are named and labeled across your company’s Helm charts.

In this scenario, you will use a MongoDB-based example to learn how to define and reuse Helm helper templates.
The focus is on improving consistency and maintainability by avoiding hardcoded names and labels.

In this task, you will:

- Generate a basic Helm chart structure.

- Define helper templates in `_helpers.tpl` for names and labels.

- Update existing Kubernetes templates to reuse those helpers instead of hardcoded values.

- Deploy the chart to verify that all resources are created with consistent names and labels.
